---
title: Connect
---

Connect covers how an assembled agent reaches beyond itself: the [[connect/model-infrastructure/index|model infrastructure]] it calls through, the [[connect/tools-and-environments/index|tools and environment interfaces]] it can act on, the [[connect/context/index|context]] and memory it carries between calls, the [[connect/knowledge-and-retrieval/index|knowledge and retrieval]] systems it draws on for information outside its training, and the [[connect/agent-communication/index|protocols it uses to reach other agents]]. Where Build fixes what an agent is, Connect determines what it can see, remember, and coordinate with while running.
